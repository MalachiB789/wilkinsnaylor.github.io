At [Wilkins Naylor Homes](../../), we are proud to support ambitious and growing local businesses across North Yorkshire. One organisation we are particularly pleased to highlight is [Pass Learn](https://passlearn.co.uk/), a rapidly expanding distance learning provider helping learners across the UK gain valuable qualifications and progress into higher education and new careers.

Pass Learn specialises in flexible online learning, offering Functional Skills Maths and English qualifications, GCSEs, and [Access to HE Diplomas](https://passlearn.co.uk/access-to-higher-education-diploma/) across a range of subject areas. Their programmes are designed to support adult learners, career changers, and individuals looking to progress into university or professional training, all through accessible and supportive remote learning.

As a North Yorkshire property business, we strongly believe in supporting the wider local economy and helping businesses thrive. At [Wilkins Naylor Homes](../../), this means more than simply providing high-quality homes. We aim to build long-term relationships with employers, organisations, and professionals who contribute positively to the region.

One of the ways we support local employers is by providing high-quality accommodation solutions for professionals who are relocating, working away from home, or travelling for work commitments. Our properties are particularly well suited to graduate employers, project-based teams, contractors, and professionals requiring comfortable and reliable accommodation during periods of transition or temporary work placements.

While we have not yet directly provided accommodation services to Pass Learn staff or learners, we would be delighted to support them in the future as the organisation continues to grow. We understand the challenges businesses can face when arranging accommodation for employees, especially when recruiting talent from outside the area or managing temporary relocations.

Our properties are maintained to a high standard and are designed to provide comfortable, practical living spaces in desirable North Yorkshire locations. Whether the requirement is for short-term accommodation, staff relocation support, or professional house shares, we work closely with employers to help provide suitable housing options that reflect the needs of modern professionals.

At [Wilkins Naylor Homes](../../), we are passionate about supporting sustainable local growth and working alongside organisations that are making a positive impact within education, employment, and the wider community.

If you are a local employer looking for high-quality accommodation for relocating or temporary staff, we would be happy to discuss how we can help. Contact [Wilkins Naylor Homes](../../contact/) today to learn more about our accommodation options and how we can support your business.
